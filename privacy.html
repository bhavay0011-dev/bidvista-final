1. Who We Are

Bidvista ("we", "our", "us") is an Amazon PPC management service. Our website is bidvista.org and we can be contacted at connect@bidvista.org or via WhatsApp at +91 86070 70505.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number when you contact us via our contact form
  • Business information you share when discussing your Amazon account (brand name, marketplace, ad spend)
  • Communications you send us via email or WhatsApp

We do not collect payment information directly — any payments are handled through third-party processors.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the Amazon PPC services you request
  • Manage your account and communicate with you about your campaigns
  • Send you information you have requested about our services
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Amazon Advertising Access

When you engage Bidvista for account management, we require access to your Amazon Advertising account. This access is limited to Campaign Manager permissions and is granted by you explicitly. We do not access any information beyond what is required to manage your campaigns. All access is revocable by you at any time through your Amazon Seller Central settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Communications containing sensitive account information are conducted through secure channels. We do not store Amazon account credentials.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies for functionality and analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect the functionality of some features.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at connect@bidvista.org.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to Amazon.com and other third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business use by adults operating Amazon seller accounts. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
